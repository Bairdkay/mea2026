Who we are

Mchinji Entertainment Awards

Mchinji Entertainment Awards (MEA) is a platform dedicated to celebrating local talent, recognising excellence, and promoting creativity across Mchinji District.

Our mission is to empower creatives, create opportunities, and expose Mchinji talent to national recognition.

Organised by

Zikomo Social Net (ZSN)

Zikomo Social Net (ZSN) is a creative and entertainment platform based in Mchinji, Malawi and Nairobi, Kenya, dedicated to promoting talent, sharing stories, and creating opportunities for creatives and young people.

ZSN is the founder, owner, and main organiser of Mchinji Entertainment Awards (MEA), an annual event celebrating local talent, recognising excellence, and promoting Mchinji creatives to the national stage.

Through digital media, entertainment, and community initiatives, ZSN aims to inspire positive change and empower local talent.

Our Mission: Bringing entertainment through words that spark change.
